## Quick Facts

- is a nonatonal language
    - each character has three main tones: high, medium, low
        - each of those main tones has a subtone: high, medium low
        - usually reffered as "main tone - subtone - character"
            - for example: "medium high a"
- has only vowels 
- phonetic inventory (without tones) is: 
    - í, ì, ı, ĩ (ji)
    - é, è, ē, ẽ (je)
    - ó, ò, ō, õ (jo)
    - á, à, ā, ã (ja)
    - ú, ù, ū, ũ (ju)
- with tones included the transcribed alphabet encompasses: 120 different letters. 
- native speakers will speak each main tone with a different specialized mouth
- one character is pronounced for exactly two seconds
- a word can't be an uneven amount of spoken time
- depending on which main tone the character has a "·" either on the upper, middle or lower left side. 
- depending on which subtone the character has a "·" either on the upper, middle or lower right side. 
- Old words stem from before the schism. 
    - They have silent consonants.
        - consonants which have been paved over by vowels. 
    - They use a seperate set of rules. 
- there is no script since celestials use psychic voice messages only. 
    - these messages can be stored as long as they are fed psychic power. 



## Grammar


### Syntax

Verb, Object, Subject


### Adjective Behaviour

Adjectives are put behind their affecting word, indicated by an adjective marker. 


### Imperative

Use the speculative present and say that someone does it. 


### Determiner

There is a marker for higher and lower celestials' names.  
There is a marker for defintive and indefinite articles.  
There is a marker for names of places or things.  


### Plural

Old words have their own seperate plural form. 
New words  have a medium low vowel as a plural indicator at the end.  
    - Some new words have only a plural form.

### Tenses


#### Speculatives

- Speculative Past: Something that might have happened or should have happened (context sensitive)
- Speculative Present: Something that might happen now or should be happening now (context sensitive)
- Speculative Future: Something that might or should happen (context sensitive)


#### Factuals

- Factual Simple Past: Something that has verifiably happened.  
- Factual Progressive Past: Something that has verifiably happened over a period of time. 
- Factual Ongoing Presently Past: Something that has verifiably happened over a period of time and is still ongoing. 


- Factual Simple Present: Something that is verifiably happening.  
- Factual Progressive Present: Something that is verifiably happening oer a period of time. 
- Factual Ongoing Presently Present: Something that has verifiably happened to someone else but is now continued by the speaker. 


- Factual Simple Future: Something that is verifiably going to happen.
- Factual Progressive Future: Something that is verifiably going to happen over a period of time. 
- Factual Ongoing Presently Future: Something that is verifiably going to happen starting now or in the past. 



### Stress

There is no stressed syllable. All are treated equal. 


### Negation

Old words jotate their last vowel.  
New words jotate their last two vowels.  


### Politeness

One is only impolite if they use the factual tenses while it is verifiable that it is wrong without using negation. 


### Punctuation

There is no punctuation. There is a symbol to mark the beginning of a sentence.  
Usually a high high vowel is used as an entry phoneme, unless it is one of the old words.
