## Physiology


### Physical and Mental Connection

Due to their intense connection to magic and nature, their physical well being and appearance is influenced by how healthy this connection is. If a fey experiences repeated heavy trauma or is cut of for a prolonged period of time, their physical bodies will start to decay and their mental faculties dim. Depending on the severity of such cases and mental recovery afterwards, a fey may be able to hide their disfigurement using magic, but doing thus would require constant active maintenance.
