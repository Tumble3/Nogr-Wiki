## Quick Facts

Coming from the continent of Undorja, the "Ajorans" are a species of great winged humanoid birds. Their society is fairly stratified along the biological differences between Ajoran subspecies. They can be divided roughly into five main sub species with distinct roles.

## Physiology


### Owl-kin

They are ruthles spies, assassins or thugs. They have eyes which can pierce the dark and wings silent like a whisper. Using either their sharp talons or a murder implement of choice they are strongly pushed into roles requiring stealth and death. 



### Finch-kin

Their small beaks and radiant colour schemes give them the broadest options in the otherwise rather strict social contract. Often seen in the entertainment and diplomatic parts of Ajoran society, the finch-kin score with their colours and voices. Most acclaimed actors and singers are finch-kin and many in the diplomatic core utilize their genetic advantage to great effect, charming others and wrapping them in a non hostile blanket. They usually grow to around 1.3 to 1.6 metres. 


### Raven-kin

The naturally black and tall raven kin usually keep to themselves as much as possible. A raven-kin normally has two options available to them, either the join the priesthood or become scholars and mages. They can grow between 2.5 and 3 metres in height and with their black or grey feathers they adapt naturally to the darker, less well lit monasteries and scholariums of the Ajoran cities. They are prone to be highly intelligent and naturally adept at magic. This is the reason why Ajoran society pushes them into becoming sages, scholars and wizards. 

### Mallee-kin

The Mallee-kin are the most valued members of Ajoran society. They have an instinctual understanding of construction, agriculture and mining, making them perfect candidates for city development roles. They are the smallest of all kin coming in at about one metre in height. 


### Eagle-kin

The Eagle-kin are the back bone of Ajoran society. They measure around 1.7 to 2 metres in height and pack powerful muscles. They are the soldiers and logisticians of Ajoran society. They keep goods and materiel flowing and face aggressors in open combat. They are the most numerous of all the kin by a small margin. They may occupy most of the less mentally challening roles in Ajoran society but given that they are what keeps everyone supplied, they are neither opressed nor looked down upon.
